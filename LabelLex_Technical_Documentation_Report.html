1. Project Overview & Statutory Motivation

1.1 Problem Statement & Background

Under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 [LM(PC) Rules, 2011], all pre-packaged commodities distributed across India must prominently display mandatory statutory declarations on their Principal Display Panel (PDP) and Information Panels. Non-compliance results in severe penal liabilities under Section 36(1) (fines ranging from ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 and compounding under Section 48).

Traditional verification workflows face critical bottlenecks:

  • Manual Inspection Bottlenecks: Enforcement officers manually verify tiny font sizes, complex manufacturer addresses, and unit sale price math across thousands of retail SKUs.
  • Consumer Information Asymmetry: Consumers struggle to verify deceptive packaging, over-stickered Maximum Retail Prices (MRP), or missing mandatory tax disclosures.
  • Pre-Market Artwork Friction: Manufacturers and packaging design agencies lack instant pre-market compliance tools to validate packaging artwork before incurring expensive mass printing runs.

LabelLex resolves this by providing an autonomous, multi-panel Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and statutory rule verification platform that audits packaging declarations in under 2 seconds.

1.2 Target Stakeholder Personas & Roles

LabelLex integrates 4 dedicated, tailored portals engineered for distinct operational requirements:

Portal / Role Target Audience Key Capabilities & Workflow
1. Consumer Scanner Everyday citizens, shoppers, consumer activists Quick mobile camera scan, instant PASS/FAIL safety verdict, 1-tap grievance filing to National Consumer Helpline with reference ID tracking.
2. Enforcement Crawler Legal Metrology state departments, E-commerce compliance wings E-commerce URL scraping pre-screening, high-volume batch artwork scanning, penalty liability calculation (₹25k–₹50k), CSV/JSON evidence export.
3. Manufacturer Tool FMCG brand owners, packers, packaging design agencies Pre-market artwork pre-screening, automated Unit Sale Price (USP) calculator, downloadable official Pre-Market Compliance Certificate.
4. Field Officer App Legal Metrology field inspectors (District/State level) Mobile inspection tool, multi-panel evidence photo attachment (shelf context, over-stickered price tags), compounding notice drafting, secure PIN login.

1.3 High-Level System Workflow

The user captures or uploads packaging photos (Front, Back, Side, or single panel). The system executes GPU-safe image optimization, runs Tesseract.js client-side OCR across candidate orientations, repairs noisy dot-matrix character anomalies, evaluates the extracted text against 8 statutory LM(PC) rules, computes font prominence and panel placement scores, and renders a forensic audit report with actionable manufacturer communication channels.

2.2 Client-Side Edge Execution Model

Unlike conventional cloud OCR architectures that transmit heavy multi-megabyte image payloads to external third-party servers (e.g. AWS Textract or Google Cloud Vision), LabelLex performs 100% of the extraction and statutory verification on-device.

Key Advantages of the Edge Architecture:
  • Zero Server Costs: Scales to millions of scans simultaneously without API token expenses or cloud compute bills.
  • Data Privacy & Trade Secrets: Pre-market packaging artwork never leaves the manufacturer's local device before launch.
  • Offline Field Readiness: Officers in remote warehouses or retail basements without cellular connectivity can perform instant statutory audits using cached Tesseract language models.

2.3 End-to-End User Transaction Sequence

  1. Slot Ingestion: User captures packaging panels via CameraModal or file picker.
  2. Downscaling & Canvas Normalization: imageOptimizer.ts rescales images to max 2048px width/height and applies contrast normalization.
  3. OCR Worker Execution: ocrPipeline.ts invokes the Tesseract WebAssembly worker, auto-correcting orientation if needed.
  4. Field Evaluation: fieldMatcher.ts maps extracted tokens to the 8 mandatory rules in rules.ts.
  5. Verdict & Metric Computation: Computes USP, Rule 7 prominence ratios, panel placement, and overall score (0–100%).
  6. State Storage & Rendering: Commits the record to sharedStore.ts and renders ComplianceReport.tsx.

4. Backend Logic Engines & Verification Algorithms

4.1 Statutory Rules Specification Engine (src/engine/rules.ts)

Defines the 8 mandatory declarations stipulated under Rule 6(1) of the LM(PC) Rules, 2011:

Rule Clause Name Legal Reference Statutory Mandate & Threshold
Rule 6(1)(a) Manufacturer / Packer / Importer Rule 6(1)(a), LM(PC) Rules 2011 Complete physical name and address with PIN code; must include prefixes like "Mfg by:", "Packed by:", or "Marketed by:".
Rule 6(1)(b) Common / Generic Name Rule 6(1)(b), LM(PC) Rules 2011 Clear generic identity of the commodity (e.g. "Soluble Coffee Powder", "Cake").
Rule 6(1)(c) Net Quantity Rule 6(1)(c) & Rule 12 Expressed in standard SI metric units (g, kg, ml, l, N, U). Non-metric units (lbs, oz) are flagged as non-standard.
Rule 6(1)(d) Month & Year of Mfg/Packing Rule 6(1)(d), LM(PC) Rules 2011 Mandatory month and year (e.g. "08/2026", "AUG 2026", "DEC/2025"). Year-only declarations are flagged for review.
Rule 6(1)(e) Maximum Retail Price (MRP) Rule 6(1)(e), LM(PC) Rules 2011 Indian Rupees (₹/Rs.) with mandatory statutory disclosure: "Inclusive of all taxes".
Rule 6(1)(f) Consumer Care Details Rule 6(1)(f), LM(PC) Rules 2011 Name, address, phone number, and email of grievance officer.
Rule 6(1)(s) Unit Sale Price (USP) 2021 Amendment Mandatory for packs > 100g/ml; calculated as MRP divided by net metric weight (rounded to 2 decimal places).
Rule 6(10) Country of Origin Rule 6(10), LM(PC) Rules 2011 Explicit declaration: "Country of Origin: India" (or foreign nation if imported).

4.2 Pattern Extraction & Field Matcher (src/engine/fieldMatcher.ts)

The field matcher executes advanced regex tokenization, character-level OCR repair, and domain-specific heuristics. Key algorithms include token sanitization, structured number correction (repairing 'O' to '0' and 'S' to '5' in numeric contexts), and statutory clause extraction.

4.3 4-Tier Compliance Status Classification Algorithm

Each field is evaluated into one of 4 official categories conforming to SIH26034 requirements:

  • VALID: Fully conforms to statutory rule, metric standard, and legal phrasing.
  • NON-STANDARD: Value is present but uses non-metric units (e.g. "5 lbs") or misses the mandatory tax disclosure (e.g. "MRP ₹120" without "incl. of all taxes").
  • REVIEW: Text is ambiguous, partially obscured, or requires manual human officer verification.
  • VIOLATION: Mandatory statutory clause is completely missing from all scanned panels.

5. Database, Persistence & Data Schemas

5.1 Comprehensive TypeScript Data Models

Data structures are strictly defined in src/engine/rules.ts:

  • ComplianceReportData: Master audit entity containing product metadata, overall score, 8 field evaluations, readability assessment, placement assessment, and supporting evidence photos.
  • FieldEvaluationResult: Clause-level result containing ruleId, extracted text, normalized value, status, confidence, and legal guidance.
  • SupportingEvidence: In-field evidentiary photos (shelf placement, over-stickered price tags) with timestamp and officer ID.

5.2 Storage Engine & Privacy Rationale

Reports are serialized into the browser's localStorage database under the key labellex_audit_reports_v2. This ensures immediate persistence across page reloads without requiring an external database cluster. Users can export reports into structured JSON or CSV spreadsheets at any time.

6. Label Scanning & Multi-Panel OCR Pipeline (Deep Dive)

6.1 7-Stage End-to-End Scan Pipeline

The scanning engine in ocrPipeline.ts and imageOptimizer.ts processes raw camera frames through a 7-stage pipeline:

  1. Image Ingestion: Ingests File/Blob from camera stream or gallery picker. Validates against 10MB size ceiling.
  2. GPU Canvas Rescaling: Clamps image dimensions to 2048px maximum width/height to prevent mobile browser WebGL/Canvas memory crashes.
  3. Auto-Orientation Analysis: Tests 0°, 90°, 180°, and 270° candidate angles against packaging vocabulary keyword density (scorePackagingKeywords) to automatically orient sideways or upside-down jars.
  4. WASM OCR Execution: Spawns a singleton Tesseract.js worker with PSM.AUTO page segmentation mode.
  5. Bounding Box & Font Extraction: Extracts individual line bounding boxes (x0, y0, x1, y1) and line pixel heights.
  6. Character & Dictionary Normalization: Executes correctStructuredNumbers to eliminate OCR confusion between numbers and letters.
  7. Multi-Panel Aggregation: Merges text streams across Front, Back, and Side panels, tagging declarations with their source panel location.

6.2 Multi-Angle Quadrant Auto-Orientation Algorithm

The pipeline tests candidate rotation angles against a dictionary of 40+ statutory keywords (e.g. manufactured, marketed, net quantity, mrp, inclusive, batch) to select the optimal angle before extracting tokens.

6.3 Rule 7 Font Size & Rule 2(h)/8 Placement Assessments

  • Rule 7 Readability Assessment: Computes the pixel height of mandatory declarations relative to the tallest reference brand header. Ratios > 25% are marked prominent, 15%–25% acceptable, and < 15% concern.
  • Rule 2(h) & Rule 8 Placement Assessment: Verifies that Net Quantity, Commodity Name, and MRP are on the Principal Display Panel (PDP / Front), while manufacturer addresses and customer care are on the Information Panel (Back/Side).

10. Key Engineering Challenges & Technical Solutions

Challenge Faced Technical Root Cause Engineered Solution in LabelLex
1. Dot-Matrix & Inkjet Print Degradation Batch dates and MRP printed using low-res dot-matrix printers on curved jars caused OCR character fragmentation (e.g. reading '0' as 'O' or '5' as 'S'). Engineered correctStructuredNumbers() in fieldMatcher.ts which dynamically repairs numbers flanked by currency symbols or date delimiters.
2. Mobile Browser GPU Memory Crashes High-resolution 48MP/108MP mobile camera photos exceeded Safari/Chrome iOS canvas memory limits. Implemented hardware-safe canvas scaling clamping max dimensions to 2048px in imageOptimizer.ts.
3. Sideways & Inverted Jar Scans Users frequently scan cylindrical cans and jars sideways, causing OCR engines to output complete gibberish. Implemented 4-quadrant rotation scoring in ocrPipeline.ts testing candidate angles against statutory packaging keywords.
4. Mandatory Tax Disclosure Detection Manufacturers frequently omit "Inclusive of all taxes", creating Section 36(1) violations. Built strict regex parser in fieldMatcher.ts that marks MRP without statutory tax disclosure as NON-STANDARD.
