Cohort Discovery Objectives & Focus Exact Questions to Ask on the Ground
1. Kirana & Supermarket Shopkeepers
(10–15 Interviews)		 Understand distributor over-stickering, customer price disputes, and fear of Legal Metrology raids. 1. "How often do distributors deliver stock with handwritten or pasted price stickers over the original MRP?"
2. "When an inspector visits your shop, what is the #1 thing they check first?"
3. "If you had a 2-second phone scan to check incoming wholesale boxes before accepting delivery, would you use it?"
2. Legal Metrology Officers / Inspectors
(3–5 In-Depth Interviews)		 Map out manual inspection bottlenecks, compounding paperwork, and evidence durability in court. 1. "How many packaging samples do you inspect daily during field market surveillance?"
2. "What percentage of compounding cases collapse due to disputed or blurred photo evidence?"
3. "Would an auto-filled seizure notice with timestamped OCR evidence save you 30 minutes per raid?"
3. Small FMCG Manufacturers & Packers
(10–15 Packaging QA Teams)		 Identify pre-print artwork approval delays, packaging recall costs, and USP calculation errors. 1. "How much money or time do you lose if a cylinder/plate print run contains a typo in the MRP or USP?"
2. "Do your designers manually calculate the Unit Sale Price for 15 different SKU weights?"
3. "Would you pay ₹999/month for an automated pre-print compliance certificate that guarantees zero LMPC fines?"
4. Everyday Consumers & Shoppers
(15–20 Supermarket Shoppers)		 Test if consumers care enough about label compliance to open an app, or if they only care when cheated on price. 1. "When buying groceries, do you ever check the 'Unit Sale Price' (₹/g) or 'Inclusive of taxes' clause?"

3. "Would you download a standalone app just to scan labels, or would you prefer this inside WhatsApp or Blinkit?"

4. Government Procurement & Official Workflow Reality

4.1 How State Legal Metrology Departments Actually Procure Software

Government departments in India do not purchase software like private corporations. Understanding their procurement mechanisms is essential if you want to turn an SIH award into a paid state contract:

  • State-Level Autonomy: While the Legal Metrology Act 2009 is central, enforcement and revenue collection are executed by State Controllers of Legal Metrology (e.g. Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh Directorates).
  • Government e-Marketplace (GeM): Any government procurement above ₹25,000 must go through GeM. Software startups must list under "Custom IT Software Services" or "GovTech SaaS" categories.
  • Pilot Programs under Rule 149 (Direct Purchase & Proprietary Certificates): State departments can initiate small-scale pilot trials (up to ₹5 Lakh–₹10 Lakh) without massive national tenders under proprietary single-source innovation exemptions.
